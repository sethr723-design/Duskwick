DUSKWICK - PC build
===================
HOW TO RUN
  Double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). No install needed.
  Keep the "js" folder next to index.html: the game's code lives there now.
  It loads the 3D library (three.js r128) from the internet on first run.
  OFFLINE: download https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js
  and save it as "three.min.js" next to index.html. The game uses it automatically.

STORY
  Since the Dawn Ember was lost, Duskwick has lived under an endless sunset.
  You are a lamplighter. Follow the story quests (marked with a star) from Oona
  in Lamplight Square out to Frostfen, the Cinderwaste and up the Gloomspire.
  Glowing LORE STONES around the world tell the town's history: walk up to read.
  Seven to find; the Book > Lore tab lists them and hints where the rest are.

CHARACTERS
  The title screen has 3 character slots (Play / Delete / New). Delete asks you to
  click twice. Book > Options > Game > Switch character returns here. Saves live in this browser,
  and older saves carry over.

NEW (v53): FROSTFEN COMES ALIVE
  - Three new villagers stand around Frostfen's square, each with a two-part quest chain
    (open once you've reached Frostfen, Lv 3-4):
      Tobin the ice-fisher: catch him some fish, then find his grandfather's lantern, lost out
        on the ice the night the cold came.
      Ilka the wolf-tender: gather tufts of wolf fur from the brambles, then follow the glowing
        pawprints to find Nip, her lost pup. Nip stays beside her afterwards (you can pat him).
      Sister Maren: relight the four old wick-posts outside the village, then survive THE LONG
        NIGHT: shadows drift in to snuff the wicks. Walk into them to burn them away, press E at
        a wick to rekindle it, and keep at least three burning until the dawn bell. Fail and you
        can try again at any wick.
  - Each chain ends with a special named reward.
  - Three hidden Frozen Caches are tucked away in Frostfen's outskirts. Look for a pale blue
    glint. Find all three for a bonus.

NEW (v52): CASTING ANIMATIONS WHILE CHARGING
  - Every attack spell now has its own stance and gathering effect while you charge it: a fireball
    cupped in your free hand (Cinderbolt), a whip of flame coiling round the staff (Flame Lash), wings
    of fire (Phoenix Dive), a small sun rising overhead (Sunfall), sparks arcing between your hands
    (Static Spark), a thunderhead over your hat (Thunderhead), an ice hammer forming above you (Frozen
    Hammer), vines spiralling up to your raised hand (Wildvine Grasp), ghost lanterns lining up behind
    you (Night Procession), and more. A good hit sends it all rushing into your lantern; a miss fizzles.
  - Party members and other players show their own spell's stance while they charge.
  - Monsters now charge up their own way before they strike: gloomlings draw shadow into their claws,
    wolves breathe frost, hounds smoulder at the jaws, golems spin ice or spit lava, wisps whirl, and
    road and dungeon bosses raise a rune circle in their move's element.

NEW (v51): KNOCK-OUT ANIMATION

    of gloom and the screen darkens at the edges. Rekindle (or a new fight) stands them back up.
  - The defeat screen now waits for the fall to finish. Party members fall the same way.

  - Monster-summon cards (Wisp, Bog Lurker, Skeleton, Mire Beast, Drowned) bring the monster's

  - Townsfolk have hair under their hats, eye whites, a trim-coloured collar and cuffs, boots

  - Linked gates: build two "Zone gate" pieces (Landmarks) in the editor and give them the same name in the Text box.

  - The tiny roadside flowers, grass tufts, pebbles and toadstools can be clicked in the editor now (one at a time,

  SOFTER STORY WARDS: the purple walls are now low drifting gloom, faint from far away.
    It thins as you work on the quest that clears it, you slow down as you wade in
    instead of hitting a wall, the hint shows once per visit, and the gloom rises and
    melts away when the quest is done.

EARLIER (7)
  CLEARER BATTLE SLOTS: every fight lays down 8 glowing pads, 4 teal shield pads for
    your team and 4 crimson horned pads for foes, numbered 1-4. Empty pads stay dim.
    Slots are staggered so nobody hides behind anyone, the camera sits higher, and
    name tags are bigger, tinted by side and carry the matching slot number. A new
    "Your team" panel on the left lists your summons and companions; foe panels on
    the right show their slot numbers too.
  WALK-INS: street foes keep patrolling during a fight. One that wanders close joins
    at the end of the round, but only if a foe pad is free and the fight would stay
    fair for your level (otherwise it waits at the edge). The same level check now
    limits how many foes and pack-mates pile in when a fight starts. The old random
    mid-fight spawns are gone.
  DUNGEONS: three instanced dungeons behind swirling doors, each with two halls, a
    sealed boss hall (the gloom ward fades once every other foe inside is down), a
    treasure chest and a boss with two minions:
      Mossy Catacombs (Lv 2) - north edge of Wispwood Glade - Mossbound Tyrant
      Rimefang Den (Lv 4)    - the wolf den in north-west Frostfen - Rimefang Matriarch
      Kiln Depths (Lv 5)     - kiln door in north-east Cinderwaste - Kiln Mother
    Foes come back each time you enter. First clear: big gold bonus and an Epic item.
    Each has a side quest, and Book > Quests shows your clears.
  BETTER SAFE ZONES: the Frostfen and Cinderwaste plazas are one big safe area around
    the shops, healer, board and gate. Both glades have a safe camp by the gate, and
    each dungeon has a safe landing. Safe ground is paved, ringed in green with little
    ward-lantern posts, and the screen edge glows green while you stand on it.
  MINIMAP AND QUEST HELPER: a round minimap (top right) turns with the camera and
    shows paths, houses, foes (gold = elite, purple = boss), portals, NPCs and a gold
    star for your tracked quest (or the way out toward it). A quest tracker card sits
    under it, and a zone plate shows where you are.
  NPCS: name plates over shopkeepers, healers and boards; a gold "!" over Oona or a
    board when there is a new quest, and "?" when one is ready to turn in.
  WORLD: big title cards when you enter an area, sky lanterns drifting over the square,
    giant glowmushrooms around Wispwood Glade, obsidian shards around Cinder Hollow.
    Book and Bag moved to the bottom right.
  CLIPPING FIXES: foe patrol routes no longer walk through carts, fire pits, wells,
    trees or stones. Trees, pines and spires are cleared away from the Frostfen lodge,
    stalls, tents, healers, boards, lamps, chests and lore stones. Glade foes moved to
    the road side of each glade.

EARLIER (6)
  TIMED HITS AND BLOCKS: when you cast an attack, a ring closes over the target.
    Press Space/Enter or click as it meets the inner circle: Good = +15%,
    Perfect = +30%, and Perfects in a row build a combo (up to +50%). When a foe
    goes for you, a blue ring closes: Block takes 40% less, a Parry takes 75%
    less, shrugs off burn/frostbite and strikes back. Missing costs nothing.
    Turn it off in Book > Quests if you prefer.
  ELITE FOES: about one street foe in six glows gold and has a trait (Armored,
    Vampiric or Frenzied). 60% more HP, double XP and gold, and a guaranteed
    Rare-or-better drop. Traits re-roll each time foes respawn.
  TREASURE CHESTS: 10 golden chests hidden on the roads, the glades, both zones
    and the Gloomspire. Each gives gold and a piece of gear.
  SNUFFED LAMPS: 8 lamps around Duskwick flicker purple. Walk up to relight them
    for XP; relight all 8 for an Epic gift. Progress is in Book > Quests.
  A ROOMIER SQUARE: Lamplight Square is wider and the clutter in the middle is
    gone (fewer pigeons and wanderers, no benches or flower beds, market moved
    to the edge).

EARLIER (5)
  GEAR: six slots (Staff, Hat, Robe, Charm, Ring, Boots) and five rarities
    (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary). Gear rolls stats: max HP, spell
    power, ward (less damage taken), crit chance, fire/ice/storm damage, healing,
    starting pips and flee chance. Foes drop gear (alphas and bosses always do,
    and drop the best). Bram, Yara and Korr sell new gear each time you level.
    Open the Bag (button or I) to equip, compare (+/- shown) and sell.
  SEAMLESS STREET FIGHTS: the camera glides into battle, you and the foes walk
    to your places from where you stood, and after a win you stay right there.
  NO MORE AMBUSHES: foes respawn only when you are far away; after a fight or a
    teleport there is a short grace period, and a foe whose ring you are already
    standing in stays calm (green ring) until you step out of it.
  COMBAT: floating damage numbers, crits, elemental weaknesses (shown on each
    foe's panel), and SPELLWEAVE: an attack of a different school than your
    previous attack that turn hits 20% harder. Foes have moves of their own:
    Dusk Imps DRAIN a pip, Moss Brutes GUARD (half damage), wolves HOWL to
    empower the pack, hounds IGNITE you.
  WORLD: richer houses and shop signs, garland lights, townsfolk who talk,
    pigeons, a cat, snow hares, a windmill, a pumpkin farm, a stream and bridge,
    kilns, a ruined watchtower, shrines, Frostfen cottages and campfire, an ice
    fisher, obsidian huts and a fallen giant's statue. Frostfen is now a dusky
    twilight instead of glaring white, and signs read correctly from both sides.

CONTROLS
  Walk:        W A S D or arrow keys      Run: hold Shift (touch: Run button)
  Turn camera: drag with the mouse, or Q / E.  Zoom: mouse wheel
  Talk:        walk up to an NPC, quest board or lore stone
  Book:        click the Book button or press B (quests, spells, party, lore, fast travel)
  Bag:         click the Bag button or press I (gear and inventory)
  Battle:      click a card or press 1-9, Space/Enter = end turn, F = flee
               Tab or click a foe (or its name tag) = choose your target
               X = toggle 1x / 2x battle speed
  Close menu:  Esc
  Fullscreen:  F while exploring, or the corner button (Esc leaves fullscreen)

FOES AND SAFE ZONES
  Foes walk fixed patrol routes up and down the streets and roads (or small loops
  in open ground). They never chase you. Each one has a red ring: step inside and
  the fight starts right there, on the street.
  Green glowing sidewalks, the square, the Frostfen/Cinderwaste plazas, the glade
  camps and dungeon landings are SAFE (paved, ringed in green, "SAFE" on the zone plate).

BATTLES (up to 4 vs 4)
  Nearby foes join in, and harder areas bring bigger packs:
    Gloomway Street up to 2, the roads up to 3, Frostfen up to 3,
    Cinderwaste and the Gloomspire up to 4, but never more than your level can
    handle. A foe that walks close during a fight joins at the end of the round
    under the same rule. Dungeon bosses and the big bosses never get walk-ins.
  Bosses bring minions sized to their strength: the Cinder Fiend has two
  Emberlings (they burn you), the Gloom Warden has three Gloom Knights (they
  heal him). Bosses also hit your whole side every few turns. No fleeing bosses.
  COMPANIONS on call fill the empty friendly spots when you are outnumbered.
  Summons take a spot too (a new summon replaces an old one when you are full).
  3 pips per turn (4 as Gale), bank up to 5. Flee chance is shown on the button.
  Combos: Chill then fire = MELT (+30). Burn then storm = OVERLOAD (+20, stun).

BUILDS
  Book > Spells: pick which of your spells your hand is drawn from.
    Slots grow with level (5 at Lv 1, up to 10). New spells for your class appear at
    Wren's as you level (a toast tells you); bought spells join your spellbook while
    there's room.
  Attunement (Pyre / Rimeward / Gale) can be switched any time in Book > Spells.
  Book > Party: choose up to 3 companions to keep on call.
    Lantern Squire (Lv 2), Hedge Witch (Summoner quest),
    Frost Pup (Wolf cull quest), Ember Pup (Ember hunt quest).
  New creature summons: Moss Golem, Lantern Owl, Wolf Spirit, Cinder Drake.
  New spells: Ice Lance, Mending Light, Ember Wave, Chain Lightning, Dusk Hex,
    Lampfall (after the Gloomspire).

GETTING AROUND FASTER
  Run with Shift. Fast travel from Book > Travel to any glade or zone you have
  visited. Accept and turn in quests straight from the Book. The next story
  quest is picked up automatically when you finish one. 2x battle speed.

WORLD
  Lamplight Square: Oona (quests), Mira (healer), Pip (tailor), Bram's market.
    North: Gloomway Street, ending at the Cinder Fiend and the Gloomspire.
    East:  Hollow Road -> Wispwood Glade -> Frostfen gate (needs level 3).
    West:  Ashen Trail -> Cinder Hollow -> Cinderwaste gate (needs level 4).
  Gloomspire (needs level 5 and a Cinder Fiend kill): three floors, Gloom Warden on top.
